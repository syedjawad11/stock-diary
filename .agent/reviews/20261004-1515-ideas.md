For restocking, your existing rule is **low when `qty <= low_threshold`**. Without adding a target-stock field, the honest calculation is:

`minimum addition to clear alert = max(0, low_threshold + 1 - qty)`

Label it **“Minimum to clear low-stock alert.”** It is not a demand forecast or an optimal purchase recommendation. For example, basmati starts at 7 with threshold 8: adding 2 clears the alert; adding 1 does not.
